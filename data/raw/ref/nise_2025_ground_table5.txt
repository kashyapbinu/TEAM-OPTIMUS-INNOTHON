The feasibility analysis yielded a total ground-mounted solar PV potential (DC capacity) of 3343.37 GWp
across India, derived from a feasible wasteland area of 27,571.39 km2, which represents approximately
6.69% of the total identified wasteland (4,12,458.37 km2). This is in line with the imposed constraint that
no more than 10% of wasteland of the state to be utilized for solar deployment.
