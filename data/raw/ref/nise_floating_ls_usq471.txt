Will the Minister of NEW AND RENEWABLE ENERGY be pleased to state: 
(a) the status and timeline for finalisation and notification of the draft Floating Solar PV 
(FSPV) Potential Assessment Report and the draft Floating Solar Policy prepared by the 
National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE) and Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee; 
(b) the total floating solar potential identified across water bodies in the country under this 
assessment, State/UT-wise including Himachal Pradesh particularly for Shimla Lok Sabha 
Constituency and Madhya Pradesh; 
(c) whether the draft policy proposes a single-window clearance mechanism for floating solar 
projects involving multiple State departments and if so, the details thereof; and 
(d) whether any targets fixed for FSPV capacity addition by the year 2030 and if so, the 
details thereof including Himachal Pradesh particularly in Shimla Lok Sabha Constituency? 
ANSWER 
THE MINISTER OF STATE FOR NEW & RENEWABLE ENERGY AND POWER 
(SHRI SHRIPAD YESSO NAIK) 
 
(a) to (d) As per the Solar PV Potential of India – Floating Solar report published by National 
Institute of Solar Energy in June 2026, India’s floating solar potential is estimated to be 102.18 
GWp. The State/UT-wise floating solar potential identified including the States of Himachal 
Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh is given at Annexure. 
  
Currently, there is no Floating Solar Policy available; however, considering the challenges 
involved in the implementation of floating solar projects, it requires coordination with state 
departments like water resources, irrigation, forest, etc., when compared to ground-mounted 
solar projects. There is no target fixed for FSPV capacity addition in the country as of now.
